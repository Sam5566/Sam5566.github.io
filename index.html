Publications

  1. [1]
    arXiv:2607.12008[pdf]astro-ph.GA

    A Universal Distribution of Dark Matter in Milky Way-like galaxies and How to Infer It

    Sam Cheng-Tse Huang, Matthew R. Buckley, Justin I. Read, David Shih

    Status
    arXiv preprint (Jul 2026)

    How dark matter is spread out and moving in the Milky Way matters for reading direct-detection experiments, but it is hard to observe. Across three independent suites of cosmological simulations of Milky Way-mass galaxies, a simple change of coordinates makes the dark matter phase-space distribution nearly the same in every galaxy; kinematic plots and machine-learning classifiers that see the full phase space both support this. It breaks down only at extreme radii or speeds, and in one simulation with a prominent accreted dark disk. The parameters of the transformation can be inferred from metal-poor stars ([Fe/H] < −2), which also carry the dark disk's signature, and a normalizing flow models the universal distribution. Applying it to Gaia and SDSS data is left for a forthcoming paper.

  2. [2]
    arXiv:2205.02064[pdf, journal]hep-ph

    Updated constraints on the Georgi–Machacek model, and its electroweak phase transition and associated gravitational waves

    Ting-Kuo Chen, Cheng-Wei Chiang, Cheng-Tse Huang, Bo-Qiang Lu

    Journal-ref
    Phys. Rev. D 106, 055019 (2022)

    An updated scan of the model's parameter space under unitarity, vacuum stability, Higgs measurements and LHC searches for exotic scalars. Among the regions that give a strong first-order electroweak phase transition, only the one-step transition survives, and most of the gravitational-wave signals it produces are within reach of proposed detectors. Di-Higgs and exotic-scalar production at the LHC can test the model.

  3. [3]
    ATLAS-CONF-2021-014[cds]hep-ex

    Measurements of gluon fusion and vector-boson-fusion production of the Higgs boson in H→WW*→eνμν decays using pp collisions at √s = 13 TeV

    ATLAS Collaboration

    Journal-ref
    ATLAS conference note (2021)

    ATLAS measures how often the Higgs boson is produced by gluon fusion and by vector-boson fusion, using its decay to an electron, a muon and two neutrinos in LHC Run 2 data. I worked on the WW control region, where a new transverse-mass definition with BDTs and DNNs raised W purity by up to 15%.

  4. [4]
    arXiv:1905.02396[pdf, journal]hep-ph

    Consistency of Gauged Two Higgs Doublet Model: Gauge Sector

    Cheng-Tse Huang, Raymundo Ramos, Van Que Tran, Yue-Lin Sming Tsai, Tzu-Chiang Yuan

    Journal-ref
    JHEP 09 (2019) 048

    The new gauge-sector parameters of the gauged two-Higgs-doublet model are constrained with LEP electroweak precision data on and off the Z pole and with LHC Drell–Yan and high-mass dilepton searches, with projections for the proposed CEPC collider. The paper also shows why the Stueckelberg mass for hypercharge must vanish to keep the standard charges of quarks and leptons.
